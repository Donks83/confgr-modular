Three directions for the customer configurator

Each one is clickable. Change the size, add a shelf, press Place… to see the snap dots, tap the product to select a part, try undo (Ctrl+Z). Adding a side rack shows the "draft, not orderable" state you chose. The product image is a real render of our own export, on the light build space. All three run on the same design tokens, so each can wear either theme:

AStudio — product left, options right, a quiet catalogue page

The closest to Kesseböhmer's own pages: square edges, light Roboto headings, a deep pine ink, generous white. Options are one scrolling column in the order you asked for (size → bays → shelves & storage → accessories → mounting), with the price, the validity state and the call to action always pinned at the foot. On a phone the product stays pinned at the top and the options scroll under it.

BFloating — the product is the whole screen

Full-bleed stage; options in a floating card with one tab per group; price, validity, parts list and the call to action in one bar at the bottom. The parts list slides in from the right. On a phone the options become a bottom sheet over the stage, with the bar beneath — the shape the runtime has today, done properly.

CGuided steps — one decision at a time, on a dark rail

A step bar across the top (Size → Bays → Shelves & storage → Accessories → Mounting → Review). The rail holds only the current step, with Back / Next; the review step is the parts list and the call to action. The rail is dark (pine in the YouK theme, today's charcoal in the neutral one), so the light build space reads as the hero.

Mockups only: no engine, a still render, made-up interactions. The decisions they should settle are layout, hierarchy and tone.
